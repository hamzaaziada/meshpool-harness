# Lessons added after later reviews (slides)

- When two authorizations overlap, state the combined ceiling and the incremental amount, not the sum of both: wrong -> "$XA remaining plus $XB renewal equals the $XC program"; right -> "$XC total ceiling, of which $XB is newly authorized and $XA remains undrawn." (2026-11-16)
- Do not let the recommendation rest on a test the deck itself disclaims; use the stated hurdle or label the size as judgment: wrong -> "EPS-accretion test, not a value test" used to justify the buyback; right -> "Buyback returns X% against the Y% cost of capital" or "size is judgment pending a value test." (2026-11-16)
- Make the recommended option's row foot to the same cash figures as the summary and bridge: wrong -> "$XB cash left for returns" minus "$YB buyback" not equalling the stated residual; right -> one reconciled residual carried into every row and title. (2026-11-16)
- Source or range any per-unit estimate that a headline decision depends on: wrong -> "about $XM per unit" with no source or range funding the plan; right -> "$XM per unit, sourced to [primary disclosure], range $X1M-$X2M." (2026-11-16)
- Score every option on the same metric; never blank the alternative while filling the recommendation: wrong -> recommended row shows a yield, alternative row shows "n/a"; right -> both rows show the same measure or both are labelled unavailable. (2026-11-16)
- Keep every bullet's text fully inside its column so no sentence is cut off or hidden behind a figure: wrong -> "...returns are about" truncated by the right-hand number; right -> shorten the bullet or widen the column so the full line and its figure are visible. (2026-11-16)
- Reconcile any buffer or floor cited as the reason for a cap to the balances it constrains, and state its dollar basis: wrong -> "$XB after the plan and $YB after the buyback against a $ZB buffer" with no arithmetic; right -> show the bridge from balance to buffer and label the buffer's basis. (2026-11-16)
- Write each next step to fit one line so no step leaves an orphan wrap: wrong -> a step ending in "renewal." or "a month"; right -> "Renew the $XB repurchase authorization" on a single line. (2026-11-16)
- Never compare an accounting accretion rate to a market yield as if they were the same kind of return: wrong -> "X% EPS accretion versus Y% T-bill yield"; right -> compare like-for-like returns or label the accretion as an accounting change, not a return. (2026-11-16)
- Carry every headline figure (renewal amount, remaining capacity) through the summary, exhibits and closing so a reader can trace it: wrong -> "approve $XB of returns" in the summary and "renew by $YB" at the close with no bridge; right -> one reconciled set of amounts on every page. (2026-11-16)
- Derive every headline return and its hurdle on the page, with inputs (price, earnings, beta, risk premium), rather than asserting two rates side by side: wrong -> "14.5% implied return vs 5.15% cost of equity" with no derivation; right -> show the earnings yield and the cost-of-equity build so the comparison is checkable. (2026-11-16)
- Compute shares retired as buyback dollars divided by the buy price, and sanity-check against share count: wrong -> "$XB retires 2.7M shares (0.6% of 443.1M)"; right -> "$XB at $X a share retires ~XM shares, X% of the count." (2026-11-16)
- Make the cash bridge deduct every use it claims to fund, so the residual equals opening cash less all outflows: wrong -> "$20.21B less $3.0B buyback = $17.2B" while also claiming to fund $7.5B capex; right -> opening cash less capex, dividend and buyback equals the stated residual. (2026-11-16)
- Show the downside case as a number, not a research item, whenever the decision depends on the trough: wrong -> "intra-year low is a research item and not shown"; right -> forecast trough cash under the plan and state the cushion against the buffer. (2026-11-16)
- Do not present a planning assumption (a total divided by an initiative count) as a disclosed data point: wrong -> "$XM per unit" sourced to a total the footnote says is not derivable; right -> label it a planning assumption with its basis and range. (2026-11-16)
- Do not plot a stock and a flow as comparable bars on one axis; label the basis or use separate panels: wrong -> "cash balance" and "annual capex" bars side by side; right -> a stock bridge plus a separate flow bar, each labelled. (2026-11-16)
- Do not strike a cap, share count or accretion off a stale or secondary market price; use a dated primary or board-date price and recompute every dependent figure: wrong -> a quote from months earlier funding the buyback; right -> the price as of the decision date, sourced, with shares retired recomputed. (2026-11-16)
- Do not label an option with a return figure that excludes part of the return; state the total on one basis: wrong -> "$XB buyback" row while the page also cites a larger total including dividends; right -> one total-return figure with its components shown. (2026-11-16)
- Do not leave a column of "n/a" in a decision table; delete it or replace it with the return test the board needs: wrong -> "Incremental ROIC: n/a" in every row; right -> the actual return or a labelled conditional gate. (2026-11-16)
- Do not set a cap against a buffer whose basis is an analyst estimate presented as a management figure; label the buffer's source and derivation: wrong -> "$XB minimum operating cash" with no disclosed basis; right -> "proposed board buffer of $XB, derived from X months of operating cash flow." (2026-11-16)
- State one decision sequence across all pages; do not ask the board to defer a decision on one page and approve it on another: wrong -> "set the ceiling only after gates clear" on the cover and "approve the conditional ceiling today" on the next page; right -> one consistent ask, either conditional approval now or deferral to a dated gate. (2026-11-16)
- Fix malformed numbers and symbols before circulating; a corrupted figure invalidates every dependent calculation: wrong -> "$$XB" and broken percentage signs in hurdle inputs; right -> "$XB" and "X%" formatted consistently throughout. (2026-11-16)
- Do not justify a decision with a test the page itself cannot support; add the valuation or incremental-economics proof or drop the claim: wrong -> "the buyback creates value only if growth persists" resting on an illustrative return versus a hurdle; right -> a supported repurchase-value test or a retitled page stating only what is shown. (2026-11-16)
- Give every policy number a stated basis and derivation, and tie any stress case to a named operating scenario rather than an arbitrary value: wrong -> a proposed buffer and return ceiling with no selection logic and a downside assuming $0 operating cash flow; right -> "buffer of $XB, derived from X months of operating cash flow" and a downside tied to a labelled scenario. (2026-11-16)
- Label what each downside and bridge figure includes and whether the proposed ceiling is incremental to ordinary dividends: wrong -> a shortfall attributed to the return ceiling when the case also includes ordinary dividends; right -> "shortfall includes $XB of ordinary dividends and $YB of proposed returns; ceiling is incremental to dividends." (2026-11-16)
- Make every headline figure ladder visibly to the figures beside it: wrong -> a rail of unlabeled amounts that do not sum to the title's number; right -> a one-line bridge showing each component adding to the headline. (2026-11-16)
- Reconcile any two figures that describe the same quantity before publishing: wrong -> a larger buyback retiring only marginally more shares than a smaller one; right -> shares retired recomputed from the buyback dollars and buy price so both rows agree. (2026-11-16)
- Use one return size across every page: wrong -> the summary promises $XB while the exhibit and next steps cap it at $YB; right -> the same amount and composition on the cover, table and close. (2026-11-16)
- Keep every bridge item in the same unit and period: wrong -> a fiscal-year spend, a balance-sheet liability and an annual dividend netted against a cash balance; right -> a dated cash bridge with each flow labelled by period and unit. (2026-11-16)
- Show the build from per-unit cost and unit count to the stated total: wrong -> a per-unit figure and a count implying a total the page never reconciles; right -> per-unit cost times units equals the total, with the buffer check tied to the same basis. (2026-11-16)
- Put the central recommendation page immediately after the cover, before any context or summary page: wrong -> executive summary and cash-context exhibit precede the ask; right -> cover, then the recommendation page, then the summary. (2026-11-16)
- Make the headline cash figure match the recommended scenario, not a larger alternative: wrong -> title says "leaving $XB of cash" while the recommended case leaves $YB; right -> title states the residual for the recommended case and labels the other scenario. (2026-11-16)
- Reconcile the opening balance to every outflow so the bridge foots exactly: wrong -> opening cash less listed uses leaves an unexplained gap; right -> opening cash less all uses equals the stated residual, with no missing amount. (2026-11-16)
- Give every buffer or floor used as a binding constraint a stated basis and derivation: wrong -> a buffer cited as the cap with no source and an analyst estimate presented as management's; right -> "buffer of $XB, derived from X months of operating cash flow." (2026-11-16)
- When two options differ only in size, the larger spend must show a strictly worse per-share outcome; never let both ranges share the same top: wrong -> "$XB and $YB both top out at $X.93"; right -> the larger option's high end is lower by the incremental funding cost. (2026-11-16)
- Show the buffer as a derived line, not an assertion: build it from the stated plan and requirement so the reader can see the arithmetic: wrong -> "$XB buffer" with no build; right -> "buffer = $XB capex plan + $YB requirement = $ZC." (2026-11-16)
- Score every live option on the metric that drives the decision, including the non-recommended one: wrong -> EPS shown only for the buybacks, blank for the dividend; right -> diluted EPS for each option or a labelled sensitivity across sizes. (2026-11-16)
- Footnote any share count or percentage derived from a price, and state the assumed average price and period: wrong -> "$XB retires XM shares (Y%)" with no price; right -> "$XB at an assumed $X a share retires ~XM shares, Y% of the count." (2026-11-16)
- Show headroom against any binding constraint for each option, not just pass/fail: wrong -> "clears the buffer" with no figure; right -> "$XB buffer less $YB residual = $ZB headroom." (2026-11-16)
- State one post-decision cash figure across the cover, summary and next steps; never let the cover and the closing show different residuals: wrong -> cover says $XB left while the next-step page says $YB; right -> one reconciled residual on every page. (2026-11-16)
- Name a ratio for what it actually measures and tie any buffer to a stated basis: wrong -> cash divided by capex called a 'coverage ratio' with a buffer sourced to an analyst estimate; right -> 'cash-to-capex multiple of Xx' and 'buffer of $XB, derived from X months of operating cash flow.' (2026-11-16)
- Put the authorization-versus-spend distinction on the summary page, not only in a footnote: wrong -> 'authorize up to $XB of returns' on the summary with the ceiling-versus-draw split buried in a footnote; right -> 'up to $XB authorized; $YB assumed drawn in the period' stated where the board reads it. (2026-11-16)
- Make the recommended row visually unmistakable in an options table: wrong -> the recommended line distinguished only by shading; right -> a boxed or labelled recommended row with its deciding number on the face of the table. (2026-11-16)
- Derive the hurdle on the page from sourced inputs and show the return on the same basis as the alternative use of cash: wrong -> an unsourced cost of equity and an EPS yield compared against it; right -> a labelled cost-of-equity build and the buyback return versus the return on the cash if not spent. (2026-11-16)
