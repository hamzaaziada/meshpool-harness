# Precedent transactions

## Screen

- Deals in the same business model, ideally in the last 5-7 years; include strategic and financial buyers and mark
  which is which. Note market conditions (rate environment, sector cycle) that make old deals less comparable.
- Use only deals with public terms: press releases, merger proxies (DEFM14A / scheme documents), 8-Ks, reputable
  press. Cite each deal's source and date. If a figure is reported by the press only, say so.

## Per deal

Date announced, acquirer, target, consideration (cash / stock / mix), equity value, enterprise value, target LTM and
NTM revenue and EBITDA at announcement, EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, premium to the unaffected price (1-day and 30-day
VWAP). Status (closed, pending, withdrawn).

## Output

- Table with median and quartiles of the multiples and of the premiums.
- Applied range for the target and the implied value per share.
- A short read-across: which deals are most relevant and why; how today's offer premium compares with the median
  premium paid.

Control premiums from precedents justify the gap between trading value and offer value; say whether synergies support
paying it.
